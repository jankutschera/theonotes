# Ideas, Logs and References

#getting started#

Every note can be one of three kinds. Decide once, and you can find the note again by its kind, without inventing a tag for it.

## The three kinds

- **Idea**: a thought or a possibility. Not a promise yet.
- **Log**: something tied to a moment, like a meeting, a decision, a day's reflection or something you learned.
- **Reference**: something you will look up later, like a recipe, instructions, a checklist, a link or an address.

A note has at most one kind. A note without a kind waits in the Inbox.

## Where they show up

Near the top of the sidebar you find **Inbox**, **Ideas**, **Logs** and **References**, each with a count. The Inbox holds the notes that have no kind yet. In the note list, a small label shows each note's kind, so a search result tells you what it is at a glance.

Capture never asks for a kind. Write first, sort later.

## Set a kind

- Open the type menu in the toolbar
- Or choose **Note ▸ Type**
- Or press ⌃⌥1 for Idea, ⌃⌥2 for Log, ⌃⌥3 for Reference, or ⌃⌥0 for Remove Type

Choosing another kind replaces the old one. With several notes selected, it applies to all of them. ⌘Z takes it back. A locked note keeps its kind until you unlock it.

## It is just a tag

The kind is stored in the note's text as a tag: `#idea`, `#log` or `#ref`. So it travels with the note when you export it. These three tags do not appear in the tag list, and only the exact tag counts: `#ref/recipes` is an ordinary nested tag.

Typing the tag by hand works too. Write `#log` at the end of a note and it is a Log. Delete the tag and the note is back in the Inbox. If a note somehow carries two of them, the first one wins.

Searching for `#ref` finds the References, and also any notes tagged `#ref/…`.

## Sort the Inbox

When the Inbox has piled up, press **Sort Inbox** at the top of the list, or choose **View ▸ Sort Inbox**. It shows one untyped note at a time, the oldest first, with its title and first lines. Open shows the whole note.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| I | Idea |
| L | Log |
| R | Reference |
| A | Archive |
| T | Trash |
| S | Skip |

Each key works straight away and moves on to the next note. ⌘Z undoes the last step. Locked notes are not offered, and read-only notes are skipped. At the end you get a short summary of what you did.

## Archive the rest

If the Inbox holds hundreds of old notes and you have a backup, you do not have to sort them all. Choose **Archive the Rest…** in the Sort Inbox window. Theo Notes tells you how many notes will move, and after you confirm, it moves all the untyped ones to the Archive in one step. They stay searchable, and you can bring any of them back. ⌘Z undoes the whole move.
