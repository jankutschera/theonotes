# Welcome to Theo Notes

#getting started#

Theo Notes is a calm place for your notes. You write in plain Markdown, find things fast, and keep what matters close.

These few notes show you around. Each one takes about a minute.

## What do you want to do?

- You want to move in from another notes app: [[Import, export and back up]]
- You want a fast place to catch a thought before it is gone: [[Capture & Next Up]]
- You want to keep a journal or a work log: [[Ideas, Logs and References]]
- You want to keep projects apart without folders: [[Tags and links]]
- You want to find an old note in seconds: [[Find anything]]
- You want some notes for your eyes only: [[Keep notes private]]
- You want the same notes on your Mac, iPhone and iPad: [[Import, export and back up/Same notes on every device|Same notes on every device]]
- You want Claude to work from your notes, as your second brain, on the Mac: [[Use Theo Notes with Claude]]

New to Markdown? [[Write and format]] shows headings, lists, to-dos and the rest.

To follow a link, hold ⌘ and click it on the Mac. On iPhone and iPad, tap it.

## Try it now

- [ ] Tap or click this box to tick it off
- [x] Tap or click this one to untick it

Start a note of your own: on the Mac press ⌘N, on iPhone and iPad tap New Note. To jump to any note by a few letters of its title, use Quick Open: ⌘O on the Mac, the magnifier button at the bottom of the note list on iPhone and iPad.

## These notes are yours

They are ordinary notes. Edit them, add your own tags, or delete them whenever you like. They will not come back by themselves.

If you want them again, on the Mac choose **Help ▸ Restore Getting Started Notes**. On iPhone and iPad, tap Library Options, then Settings, then **Restore Getting Started Notes**. Only the missing ones are added, and nothing you wrote is touched.
