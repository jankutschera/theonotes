# Ideas, Logs and References

#getting started#

You want to keep a journal or a work log, apart from your ideas and the things you look up. Give each note a kind, and you find it again by its kind. This note shows how.

## The three kinds

- **Log**: something tied to a moment, like a meeting, a decision, a day's reflection or something you learned.
- **Idea**: a thought or a possibility. Not a promise yet.
- **Reference**: something you will look up later, like a recipe, instructions, a checklist, a link or an address.

A note has at most one kind. A note without a kind waits in the Inbox.

## Start a log

Write the day's note and put the tag `#log` at the end. Now it is a Log. [[Example log: planning call, 2026-11-03]] shows one.

For one note a day, make a template once: a note with the title `{{date}} {{weekday}}`, the questions you want to answer each day, and the tags `#log` and `#template`. Then, on the Mac, choose **File ▸ New Note from Template…** and pick it. On iPhone and iPad, tap From Template at the bottom of the note list, pick it and tap Create. Today's note is ready, with today's date.

## Where they show up

Near the top of the sidebar you find **Inbox**, **Ideas**, **Logs** and **References**, each with a count. In the note list, a small mark shows each note's kind, so a search result tells you what it is at a glance.

Capture never asks for a kind. Write first, sort later.

## Set a kind

- On the Mac, open the type menu in the toolbar, or choose **Note ▸ Type**
- On the Mac, press ⌃⌥1 for Idea, ⌃⌥2 for Log, ⌃⌥3 for Reference, or ⌃⌥0 for Remove Type
- On iPhone and iPad, tap Note Actions, choose **Note Info**, and pick the kind under Type

Choosing another kind replaces the old one. On the Mac it applies to all the notes you selected, and ⌘Z takes it back. A locked note keeps its kind until you unlock it.

## It is just a tag

The kind is stored in the note's text as a tag: `#idea`, `#log` or `#ref`, so it travels with the note when you export it. These three tags do not appear in the tag list, and only the exact tag counts: `#ref/recipes` is an ordinary nested tag. Delete the tag and the note is back in the Inbox. If a note carries two of them, the first one wins.

## Sort the Inbox

When the Inbox has piled up, press **Sort Inbox** at the top of the list (on the Mac you can also choose **View ▸ Sort Inbox**). It shows one untyped note at a time, the oldest first, with its title and first lines. Open (Open Note on iPhone and iPad) shows the whole note.

On the Mac, or with a keyboard attached, one key does each step. On iPhone and iPad you can also tap the buttons.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| I | Idea |
| L | Log |
| R | Reference |
| A | Archive |
| T | Trash |
| S | Skip |

Each key or button works straight away and moves on to the next note. On the Mac, ⌘Z undoes the last step. Locked notes are not offered, and read-only notes are skipped. At the end you get a short summary of what you did.

## Archive the rest

Hundreds of old notes in the Inbox, and a backup made? Choose **Archive the Rest…** in Sort Inbox. Theo Notes tells you how many notes will move, and after you confirm, it moves all the untyped ones to the Archive in one step. They stay searchable, and you can bring any of them back. On the Mac, ⌘Z undoes the whole move.
