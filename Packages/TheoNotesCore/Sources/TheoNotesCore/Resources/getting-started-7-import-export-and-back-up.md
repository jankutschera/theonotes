# Import, export and back up

#getting started#

You want to move in from another notes app, have the same notes on all your devices, and never lose them. This note shows how, in that order.

## Bring your notes in

On the Mac, choose **File ▸ Import Notes…** and pick files or folders: Markdown, text, HTML, RTF, TextBundle and more. A folder of Markdown files, such as an Obsidian vault, comes in the same way. For notes from other apps, use **File ▸ Import From**:

- **Import From ▸ Bear…** brings a library over from Bear
- **Apple Notes…** reads the Notes app directly. macOS asks you for permission first
- **Apple Notes Export…** takes the folder you saved from Notes with File ▸ Export as ▸ Markdown
- **Evernote…** takes the export files (.enex) that Evernote writes
- **Notion…** takes the zip file from Notion's workspace export, or the folder you unpacked it into

On iPhone and iPad, tap Library Options, then **Import Notes**, and pick files: Markdown, text, HTML or TextBundle. Importing from other apps is a Mac feature.

The import window shows what it found. Titles, dates and tags come along where the other app has them.

## Same notes on every device

Theo Notes keeps your notes in step through your own iCloud. Turn it on in the app on each device:

- On the Mac: Settings ▸ iCloud ▸ **Sync with iCloud**
- On iPhone and iPad: Library Options ▸ Settings ▸ iCloud ▸ **Sync with iCloud**

Use the same iCloud account on every device, with iCloud Keychain on: it carries the key to your notes, which are encrypted before they leave the device. A backup is saved before the first sync. The first device offers you 24 recovery words. Write them down and keep them apart from your devices: they get your notes back if you lose every device. If you have locked notes, Theo Notes asks for your Note Password to start syncing.

## Back up

On the Mac, choose **File ▸ Backup Notes…** to save everything in one file: notes, pictures, files, tags and pins. Locked notes stay locked inside it. **File ▸ Restore Backup…** reads it back: Merge adds what is missing, and Replace All swaps your library for the backup after it asks you and saves a safety copy of your current notes.

On iPhone and iPad, Settings ▸ Advanced ▸ Backup and Restore has Create Backup and Choose Backup to Restore, with the same choice.

The Mac also keeps a daily copy of the database. Settings ▸ Advanced shows when the last one was made and where the folder is.

## Take your notes out

On the Mac, choose **File ▸ Export Notes…** to save the selected notes, or a whole tag, as Markdown, TextBundle, plain text, HTML, PDF or Rich Text. Pictures and files go with them. **Edit ▸ Copy As** copies a note as Markdown, plain text, HTML or rich text, and **File ▸ Print…** prints it.

On iPhone and iPad, open the note and tap Note Actions: **Export** has the same formats and Print, **Copy As** the same choices as on the Mac, and Share Markdown sends the text to another app.

A switch in Settings ▸ General decides whether tags stay in exported files.
