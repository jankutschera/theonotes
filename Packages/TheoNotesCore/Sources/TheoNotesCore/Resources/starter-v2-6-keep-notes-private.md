# Keep notes private

#getting started#

You want some notes for your eyes only, or the whole app closed to anyone who picks up your device. Lock single notes, or lock the app. This note shows both.

## Lock a note

On the Mac, select a note and choose **Note ▸ Privacy ▸ Lock Note**. On iPhone and iPad, open the note, tap Note Actions and choose **Lock Note**. The first time, you pick a Note Password. One password protects all your locked notes. The text of a locked note is encrypted, and the note list shows its title only.

- Open a locked note and Theo Notes asks for the password
- To stop asking, switch on Touch ID in Settings ▸ Privacy. On iPhone and iPad the switch reads Unlock Notes with Face ID, or with Touch ID on a device that has it
- Locked notes close again when you leave Theo Notes: on iPhone and iPad right away, on the Mac after the delay you choose in Settings ▸ Privacy, and whenever the Mac sleeps. On the Mac, **Note ▸ Privacy ▸ Lock Now** closes them right away. On iPhone and iPad, Note Actions ▸ **Close Locked Notes** does the same
- **Remove Lock from Note** turns a note back into a normal one. On iPhone and iPad it is **Remove Note Lock** in Note Actions

Keep your Note Password somewhere safe. A locked note cannot be opened without it.

## Lock the app

In Settings ▸ Privacy, switch on "Require Touch ID or your Mac password to open Theo Notes" on the Mac, or "Require Authentication to Open Theo Notes" on iPhone and iPad. Then your notes stay hidden until you unlock.

On the Mac, every window stays hidden until you unlock. It locks again after the time you choose, and whenever the Mac sleeps or locks. With App Lock on, reminders show no note text.

On the Mac, good to know: the `theonotes` command and the Claude connection read your library directly, so App Lock does not stop them. See [[Use Theo Notes with Claude]] for how to limit what they can reach.
