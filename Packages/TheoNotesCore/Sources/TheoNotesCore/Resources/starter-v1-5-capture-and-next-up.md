# Capture & Next Up

#getting started#

Capture & Next Up is for the moment a thought shows up and you do not want to stop what you are doing. Catch it first. Sort it out later.

## Capture without filing

Choose **File ▸ Capture Thought**. A new note opens with the cursor ready. Type, then close the window. That is all: no title, no tag, no place to choose. If you close it without typing anything, it goes to the Trash by itself.

Captured notes get a small tray mark in the note list, so you can find them again.

You can also catch things from other apps. Choose Theo Notes in a Share menu and press Save. If you do not see Theo Notes there, switch it on in System Settings under Login Items & Extensions.

## Voice notes on the Mac

Choose **File ▸ Capture Voice Note** and start talking. Press Stop when you are done. You get a note with the words written out and the recording attached. Cancel throws it away.

The first time, macOS asks for permission to use the microphone, and Theo Notes may offer to download Apple's speech model for your language. The words are written out on your Mac.

Inside a note, **Edit ▸ Insert Voice Note** puts what you say at the cursor.

## One next step

Choose **Note ▸ Next Up**, or use its button next to Info in the toolbar. It does not show a list. It shows one thing: a to-do from your notes, or a captured note you have not looked at yet.

- Open takes you to the note, right at that line
- Done ticks the box
- Later brings up the next thing instead
- Not Today hides Next Up until midnight
- 2-Minute Timer gives you two minutes to just start

When nothing is waiting, it says so. Right now it may be showing you the box in [[Welcome to Theo Notes]].

## One reminder

Choose **Note ▸ Remind Me…** to be reminded about a note, or about the line your cursor is on. Pick a time and a banner shows up then. Click it to land right on that line, or snooze it for an hour. The first time, macOS asks whether Theo Notes may send notifications.

## Note history

Theo Notes keeps earlier versions of a note while you work. Choose **Note ▸ Note History…** to look through them. Restore as Copy opens an old version as a new note. Replace This Note… brings an old version back and keeps the current text in the history, so nothing is lost either way.
