# Capture & Next Up

#getting started#

You want a fast place to catch a thought before it is gone, without stopping what you are doing, and one clear next step when you are ready. Capture & Next Up does both. This note shows how.

## Capture without filing

On the Mac, press the Capture Thought shortcut (Control-Option-Command-N unless you changed it in Settings ▸ Capture), or choose **Capture Thought** in the Theo Notes menu bar item (switch it on with Show in menu bar on the same Settings page). A new note opens with the cursor ready. Type, then close the window. That is all: no title, no tag, no place to choose. If you close it without typing anything, it goes to the Trash by itself.

On iPhone and iPad, add the Thought control to Control Center or the Lock Screen, or put the New Note widget on your Home Screen. Both open a new note, ready for typing.

Captured notes get a small tray mark in the note list, so you can find them again.

You can also catch things from other apps. Choose Theo Notes in a Share menu (the share sheet on iPhone and iPad) and press Save. On the Mac, if you do not see Theo Notes there, switch it on in System Settings under Login Items & Extensions.

## Voice notes

On the Mac, choose **File ▸ Capture Voice Note** and start talking. Press Stop when you are done. You get a note with the words written out and the recording attached. Cancel throws it away.

The first time, macOS asks for permission to use the microphone, and Theo Notes may offer to download Apple's speech model for your language. The words are written out on your Mac.

On the Mac, inside a note, **Edit ▸ Insert Voice Note** puts what you say at the cursor.

On iPhone and iPad, add the Voice control. It opens the Voice Note sheet, and Stop Recording ends it.

## One next step

On the Mac, choose **Note ▸ Next Up**, or use its button next to Info in the toolbar. It does not show a list. It shows one thing: a to-do from your notes, or a captured note you have not looked at yet.

- Open takes you to the note, right at that line
- Done ticks the box
- Later brings up the next thing instead
- Not Today hides Next Up until midnight
- 2-Minute Timer gives you two minutes to just start

When nothing is waiting, it says so. Right now it may be showing you the box in [[Welcome to Theo Notes]].

On iPhone and iPad, Next Up is a widget: add it to your Home Screen to see your next reminder, to-do or thought.

## One reminder (Mac)

On the Mac, choose **Note ▸ Remind Me…** to be reminded about a note, or about the line your cursor is on. Pick a time and a banner shows up then. Click it to land right on that line, or snooze it for an hour. The first time, macOS asks whether Theo Notes may send notifications.

## Note history (Mac)

On the Mac, Theo Notes keeps earlier versions of a note while you work. Choose **Note ▸ Note History…** to look through them. Restore as Copy opens an old version as a new note. Replace This Note… brings an old version back and keeps the current text in the history, so nothing is lost either way.
