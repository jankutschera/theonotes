# Keep notes private

#getting started#

There are two ways to protect your notes: lock the notes that matter, or lock the whole app.

## Lock a note

Select a note and choose **Note ▸ Privacy ▸ Lock Note**. The first time, you pick a Note Password. One password protects all your locked notes. The text of a locked note is encrypted, and the note list shows its title only.

- Open a locked note and Theo Notes asks for the password
- To stop asking, switch on Touch ID in Settings ▸ Privacy
- Locked notes close again after the delay you choose there, and whenever the Mac sleeps. **Note ▸ Privacy ▸ Lock Now** closes them right away
- **Remove Lock from Note** turns a note back into a normal one

Keep your Note Password somewhere safe. A locked note cannot be opened without it.

## Lock the app

In Settings ▸ Privacy, switch on "Require Touch ID or your Mac password to open Theo Notes". Then every window stays hidden until you unlock. It locks again after the time you choose, and whenever the Mac sleeps or locks. With App Lock on, reminders show no note text.

Good to know: the `theonotes` command and the Claude connection read your library directly, so App Lock does not stop them. See [[Use Theo Notes with Claude]] for how to limit what they can reach.
