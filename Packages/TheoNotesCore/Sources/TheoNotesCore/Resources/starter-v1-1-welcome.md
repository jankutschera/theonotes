# Welcome to Theo Notes

#getting started#

Theo Notes is a calm place for your notes. You write in plain Markdown, find things fast, and keep what matters close.

These few notes show you around. Each one takes about a minute.

## Take the tour

- [[Write and format]]: headings, lists, to-dos, code and more
- [[Tags and links]]: sort notes without folders and connect them
- [[Find anything]]: search, Quick Open and special searches
- [[Capture & Next Up]]: catch a thought now, get one next step later
- [[Keep notes private]]: lock single notes, or the whole app
- [[Import, export and back up]]: bring notes in, take them out, stay safe
- [[Use Theo Notes with Claude]]: let Claude read and write your notes

## Try it now

- [ ] Click this box to tick it off
- [x] Click this one to untick it

Press ⌘N to start a note of your own. Press ⌘O and type a few letters of a title to jump to any note.

## These notes are yours

They are ordinary notes. Edit them, add your own tags, or delete them whenever you like. They will not come back by themselves.

If you want them again, choose **Help ▸ Restore Getting Started Notes**. Only the missing ones are added, and nothing you wrote is touched.
